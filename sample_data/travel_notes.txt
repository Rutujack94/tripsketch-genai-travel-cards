Munnar
Munnar is a hill station in Kerala, famous for its tea gardens and cool, misty weather. The best time to visit is between September and March, when the days are pleasant and the hills are green. Visitors can walk through the tea estates, visit the tea museum and enjoy the viewpoints. Mornings and evenings can be cold, so a light jacket is useful.

Goa Beaches
Goa is a coastal state known for its sandy beaches, seafood and old churches. North Goa is lively, with markets, beach shacks and nightlife. South Goa is calmer and better for people who want quiet beaches. Beaches are busiest in December and January, so early mornings are more peaceful.

Amber Fort, Jaipur
Amber Fort is a hilltop fort near Jaipur, built with red sandstone and marble. It was built in the 16th century by Rajput rulers. Visitors can climb up to the fort or take a ride up the slope. Going early in the morning helps to avoid the heat and the crowd.
